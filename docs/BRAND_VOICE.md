# Lumi — Brand Voice

Single source of truth for anyone (human or AI) writing Lumi copy: landing
pages, prompt packs, social posts, emails, App Store text. Derived from the
live site copy as of July 2026.

## Personality

If Lumi were a person: a working agent's sharpest colleague — someone who has
done a hundred showings, hates paperwork with a passion, explains things in
one breath, and never wastes your time. Not a vendor. Not a guru.

## Audience

Working real estate agents (solo, small teams, rookies) in EU · LatAm · MENA.
They are busy, allergic to corporate speak, live in WhatsApp and their car,
and judge tools by "does this save me time *today*". English copy, simple
vocabulary — many readers are non-native speakers.

## Voice attributes

**Direct**
- We are: short sentences, verbs first, zero filler.
- We are not: rude or clipped to the point of coldness.
- Sounds like: "Say it. Lumi does it."
- Not like: "Leverage AI-powered productivity solutions."

**Practitioner, not vendor**
- We are: talking from the field — showings, silent buyers, HOA docs,
  Sunday-evening pipeline dread. Specifics over abstractions: times of day,
  deal sizes, street names.
- We are not: pretending to be an agent; we build for them.
- Sounds like: "Log the visit in 6 seconds — hold the mic, say what happened."
- Not like: "Revolutionize your workflow with cutting-edge automation."

**Honest to a fault**
- We are: transparent about beta status, pricing, what's not built yet.
  "Coming soon" over fake screenshots. Two emails, that's it.
- We are not: hype, inflated numbers, dark patterns. Every number in our copy
  must be real or clearly qualified ("in our tests").
- Sounds like: "We'll email you twice: once when beta opens, once when we
  launch. That's it."
- Not like: "Join 50,000 top producers crushing their GCI!"

## Messaging pillars

1. **Chat-first, forms are the fallback** — every path starts as a message.
2. **One app** — calendar + CRM + documents; stop losing leads between tabs.
3. **Built for the field** — voice capture, offline-first, works at open houses.
4. **Honest deal** — €9/month after a 7-day trial, everything included,
   free during beta. No tiers, no "contact sales".

## Tone by channel

- Landing / packs: confident, concrete, second person ("you"), present tense.
- Social (IG/Threads/X): punchier, one idea per post, hook in first line,
  never more than one exclamation mark.
- Email: personal, plain, action-first; subject lines under 6 words.
- Errors / support: calm, blame-free, always with a way forward.

## Style rules

- Sentence case for headings. Oxford comma. Contractions welcome.
- Numbers as numerals ("6 seconds", "3 leads"). € before amount, no space.
- Em dash without spaces in English copy.
- No emoji in product/landing copy; sparing emoji OK in social and Telegram.
- No ALL CAPS except tiny mono labels (design system eyebrows).

## Terminology

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| agent | realtor® (trademark), salesperson |
| showing | viewing (US-lean copy), tour |
| pipeline | funnel (that's marketing-speak) |
| waitlist | wait list, wishlist |
| prompt pack | cheat sheet, hack |
| Lumi (never "the Lumi") | LUMI, lumi mid-sentence |

## Hard rules

- Never claim "free forever" — pricing is €9/month after trial; beta is free.
- Never use unqualified metrics. Real number or "in our tests" / drop it.
- No "Powered by <model>" branding. Naming Claude/ChatGPT as tools inside
  prompt-pack instructions is fine.
- No company-location branding (the "Helsinki" era is over); legal
  jurisdiction lines in Terms stay.
- Competitor mentions: factual, dated ("pricing as of mid-2026"), never
  disparaging.
